Our 3-Step Splicing Process

Effortless 3-step procedural method ensuring 100% mechanical joint reliability on active high-rise construction sites.

1

Step 1: Threading on Site

The two rebars are peeled and rolled thread on site as per ISO metric standards, and an appropriate high-tensile coupler is supplied.

2

Step 2: Coupler Bolting

The coupler is screwed and bolted onto the already fixed rebar member securely up to the mid-length stop point.

3

Step 3: Fastening & Lock

The free connecting rebar is fastened into the other end of the coupler and torque-tightened to complete the full tensile continuity splice.

Site & Labor Efficiency
  • Easy to install: Simple mechanical wrench tightening with no specialized welding required.
  • Reduces labor cost: Fewer workers required for faster column and beam splicing.
  • Reduces steel consumption: Eliminates 50D overlap steel wastage entirely.
Structural & Safety
  • Simple & robust design: Concentric load transfer without offset bending moments.
  • Safety over lap joint: Guaranteed parent bar break strength under tensile pull.
  • Reduced cycle time: Fast slab casting cycle with zero rebar congestion.
